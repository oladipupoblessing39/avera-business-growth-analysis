# Avera Business Growth Analysis

## Business Growth & Resource Prioritization Analysis

### Business Context

Avera is a savings and investment business that experienced substantial growth across customers, customer activity, financial balances, and Revenue over a 24 month period from September 2024 to August 2026.

Management needed to understand where this growth was coming from and where limited resources should receive additional focus over the next 12 months to support sustainable growth and Revenue.

The key decision was therefore not simply how to grow, but where to focus resources as the business grows.

### Business Question

Where should Avera prioritize its limited resources over the next 12 months to support sustainable growth and Revenue?

### Analytical Questions

The analysis examined:

1. Where growth was happening
2. Whether growth was creating financial value
3. Whether growth was sustained across the observed period
4. Which products contributed most to financial growth
5. Which customer segments contributed most to Revenue growth
6. Where product and customer segment performance created opportunities for further strategic attention

### Key Findings

Avera experienced substantial growth across the business over the 24 month period.

Active Customers increased from 541 to 22,760, representing approximately 4,107% growth.

Customer Activity increased from 1,268 to 72,500 transactions, representing approximately 5,618% growth.

Revenue increased from approximately ₦165,518 to ₦26.55 million, representing approximately 15,940% growth.

Total Balance increased from approximately ₦122.4 million to ₦24.65 billion, representing approximately 20,042% growth.

Across the full period, Avera recorded approximately 692K transactions, generated approximately ₦170M in Revenue, and recorded approximately ₦195bn in Total Balance across the monthly observations.

Revenue grew substantially faster than Customer Activity, showing that the growth was accompanied by a much larger increase in financial value.

There were no month to month declines in Customer Activity, Revenue, or Total Balance during the observed period. However, the pace of growth varied, so historical performance does not establish that the same growth rate will continue indefinitely.

### Product Contribution

Investment products were the strongest contributors to Avera's financial growth.

Across the full period:

1. Managed Portfolio generated approximately ₦48M Revenue
2. Dollar Investments generated approximately ₦31M Revenue
3. Naira Mutual Funds generated approximately ₦26M Revenue
4. Regular Savings generated approximately ₦4M Revenue
5. Emergency Plan generated approximately ₦3M Revenue
6. Savings Circle generated approximately ₦2M Revenue

Managed Portfolio, Dollar Investments, and Naira Mutual Funds together contributed approximately 93.2% of the increase in Total Balance across established products and approximately 91.9% of Revenue growth.

Customer Activity growth was broader across the established product range, making financial contribution an important consideration alongside activity.

### Customer Segment Contribution

All four customer segments contributed to growth, but their financial contribution differed.

Across the full period:

1. Affluent Investors generated approximately ₦86M Revenue
2. Established Earners generated approximately ₦52M Revenue
3. Early Career Earners generated approximately ₦21M Revenue
4. Young Professionals generated approximately ₦11M Revenue

Affluent Investors and Established Earners recorded the largest absolute increases in Revenue and together accounted for approximately 77.5% of the increase in Revenue.

### Hypotheses

**H1: Investment products contribute more Revenue growth than savings products.**

**Result: Supported.**

Established investment products accounted for approximately 91.9% of Revenue growth.

**H2: Revenue growth is concentrated among Affluent Investors and Established Earners.**

**Result: Supported.**

Affluent Investors and Established Earners recorded the largest absolute increases in Revenue.

### Opportunity Assessment

The analysis did not assess opportunities using a single measure. It combined:

1. Revenue growth
2. Total Balance growth
3. Recent growth momentum

The strongest product and customer combinations identified were:

1. Affluent Investors and Managed Portfolio
2. Affluent Investors and Dollar Investments
3. Established Earners and Managed Portfolio

These combinations showed strong Revenue and Total Balance growth while recent monthly performance remained positive.

### NG Stocks

NG Stocks was introduced in March 2026 and was therefore assessed separately from March to August 2026.

During this six month period:

1. Total Balance increased approximately 395.7%
2. Customer Activity increased approximately 208.7%
3. Revenue increased approximately 218.8%
4. Revenue generated approximately ₦55.04M
5. August 2026 Revenue was approximately ₦14.40M

NG Stocks showed strong early performance and quickly became an important contributor to Revenue. Because it has only six months of history, continued development should be accompanied by monitoring to determine whether this performance persists over a longer period.

### Strategic Considerations

The evidence supports greater strategic attention toward investment products, particularly Managed Portfolio, Dollar Investments, and Naira Mutual Funds, and toward Affluent Investors and Established Earners.

NG Stocks should continue to be developed selectively while its longer term performance is monitored.

The wider customer base should continue to be supported because growth occurred across all four customer segments. The recommendation is to prioritize areas with stronger financial contribution rather than stop supporting lower contribution products or segments.

### Measurement

Avera should continue monitoring:

1. Active Customers
2. Customer Activity
3. Total Balance
4. Revenue
5. Investment Product Contribution
6. Revenue by Customer Segment
7. NG Stocks Revenue

These measures should be reviewed regularly rather than relying only on the historical 24 month trend.

### Expected Business Outcomes

The intended outcomes are:

1. Greater strategic focus on products demonstrating stronger Revenue and Total Balance contribution
2. Deeper development of investment product opportunities among Affluent Investors and Established Earners
3. Continued evaluation of emerging opportunities such as NG Stocks
4. More evidence based resource decisions using Revenue, Total Balance, Customer Activity, product performance, and customer segment performance

### Important Limitation

The analysis supports strategic attention based on historical growth and financial contribution. It does not prescribe a specific budget or staffing allocation.

A specific resource allocation would require additional information such as costs, expected returns, and available resources.

Historical growth does not guarantee future performance, and the analysis identifies relationships and patterns rather than proving that one factor directly caused another.
